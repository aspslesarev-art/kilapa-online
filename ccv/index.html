How it works
A familiar pain

Grab a file. Drag it across screens.
It works. Or it doesn't.

The window's closed. Spaces switched to the wrong one. You dropped the file along the way. Back you go, dragging it again. Fifty little micro-dances, every single day.

How it works

Three gestures. No windows.

01
Drop it — into the notch
Drag a file to the top of the screen. The shelf catches it and holds on. Drop several at once — they gather into a group.
02
Switch — anywhere
Another Space, another app, another monitor, another project. Your files wait. Nothing gets lost.
03
Pull it out — wherever you need
Move your cursor to the notch and the shelf opens. Drag the file into any window: Telegram, email, chat, a form. Done.
